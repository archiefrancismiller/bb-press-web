A lonesome, insolvent crawdad sets out to topple the loan sharks and cruel robber barons who run the Bayou in this surreal 8-bit action roguelike.

Bullet Hell meets Breakout. Build with 50 unique weapons, 200 upgrades, and an array of buddies. Connect with vagrant hustlers over games of skill and chance. Keep yourself afloat in the Bayou's predatory economy. Risk it all at the casino! Save, invest. Put bold parlays on your own performance in combat!

Free demo on Steam, Newgrounds, and GX Games. Action roguelike, top-down shooter, boss rush, Game Boy, retro '90s, pixel art. Coming to Steam in 2026.
