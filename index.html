A lonesome, insolvent crawdad sets out to topple the loan sharks and cruel robber barons who run the Bayou in this surreal 8-bit action roguelike.

Trailer

Free demo available now on Steam, Newgrounds, and GX Games. Bleu Bayou launches on Steam in 2026.

History

In 2020, an early prototype of Bleu Bayou took #1 Fun in GBJAM-8. In 2025, we were drawn back to its lightning-in-a-bottle fun factor, and have overhauled and expand it into our first commercial release.

About Us

BokoYoss is three friends making games. We cut our teeth on the game jam circuit for years, and a prototype of Bleu Bayou was one of our earliest jam submissions.
